隐私政策

乐谱 Yuepu（Podium）· 最后更新：2026 年 8 月 31 日

一句话概括：我们不收集你的任何数据。

App Store 上这个 App 的隐私标签是「未收集数据」。本页说明的每一条，都与那份标签一致。

我们收集哪些数据

不收集。乐谱 Yuepu 没有我们运营的服务器，也不接入任何数据分析、崩溃统计、广告或追踪 SDK。你打开了哪些谱子、画了什么、练了多久，都不会被上报给开发者或任何第三方。

你的数据存储在哪里

  • 你导入的乐谱、标注、歌单与练习记录，存储在你的设备本地；
  • 若你登录了 Apple 账户并开启 iCloud，这些数据会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 私有数据库（CloudKit）在你自己的设备之间同步。该数据库归属于你的 Apple 账户，受 Apple 的加密与隐私保护，开发者没有任何途径读取其中的内容；
  • 删除 App 即删除本机数据；iCloud 中的数据可在系统设置的 iCloud 存储管理里删除。

关于订阅

Podium Pro 通过 Apple 的 App 内购买销售。付款全程在 Apple 的系统界面中完成，我们收不到、也无法存储任何支付信息——没有卡号，没有账单地址，没有交易记录。

App 判断你是否拥有订阅权益，完全由设备本地的 StoreKit 完成，结果只保存在本机。我们没有服务器来记录谁订阅了、订阅了多久。关于 Apple 如何处理购买信息，请参阅 Apple 自己的隐私政策。

关于登录

App 提供「通过 Apple 登录」，用来在这台设备上建立你的演奏者身份。登录是可选的，跳过登录不影响任何功能。这些身份资料只保存在你的设备本地，不会被发送到任何服务器——我们没有服务器可以接收它们。你可以在「设置 › 账户」里随时删除账户，一次抹掉本机的全部身份资料；曲库与标注不受影响，因为它们从不属于账户。

关于相机

相机只用于扫描纸质乐谱：拍下的画面在设备上直接处理成 PDF，存进你自己的曲库。图像不会上传到任何地方，App 也不会以任何其他理由使用相机。首次扫描时系统会向你征求相机权限，拒绝也不影响 App 的其他功能。

关于麦克风

麦克风只用于调音器听音：打开调音器的听音功能时，App 会分析你演奏的音高，实时显示音准。这些音频只在设备内存里即时分析，不录制、不写入文件、也不上传——我们没有服务器可以接收它。关掉调音器，采集立即停止。拒绝麦克风权限不影响 App 的其他功能，调音器仍然可以播放标准音。

关于 IMSLP

App 内的 IMSLP 目录检索使用打包在 App 里的本地索引，检索过程完全不联网，你搜了什么不会离开你的设备。

只有当你主动点击某个条目去下载时，App 才会打开 imslp.org 的页面。那是你与 IMSLP 之间的直接访问，遵循 IMSLP 自己的隐私政策与使用条款；我们不经手、不缓存、不镜像他们的文件，也拿不到你在那边的任何行为记录。下载完成后，文件由你的设备收进本地曲库。

第三方服务

App 仅使用 Apple 提供的系统服务（iCloud / CloudKit 同步、App 内购买）。没有第三方分析 SDK，没有广告，没有任何形式的追踪，也没有第三方账号体系。

用户之间的数据

App 不提供用户之间的分享、发现、社区或交易功能。你的曲库和标注严格私有，其他用户无从发现，也无法获取。

儿童隐私

由于我们不收集任何数据，App 也不会收集儿童的任何信息。

政策变更

若未来版本引入需要说明的数据处理行为（例如可选的协作功能），我们会在此页面更新说明，并在 App 更新说明中明确提示。

Privacy Policy

Podium (乐谱 Yuepu) · Last updated 31 August 2026

In one line: we collect none of your data.

This app's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected". Everything on this page is consistent with that label.

What we collect

Nothing. Podium has no server operated by us, and includes no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDK of any kind. Which scores you open, what you write on them and how long you practise are never reported to the developer or to any third party.

Where your data lives

  • The scores you import, along with your annotations, setlists and practice records, are stored locally on your device;
  • If you are signed in to your Apple Account with iCloud enabled, that data syncs between your own devices through Apple's iCloud private database (CloudKit). That database belongs to your Apple Account and is protected by Apple's encryption and privacy safeguards — the developer has no way of reading its contents;
  • Deleting the app deletes the local data. Data held in iCloud can be removed from iCloud storage management in system Settings.

Subscriptions

Podium Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment happens entirely within Apple's own interface, and we neither receive nor store any payment information — no card number, no billing address, no transaction record.

Whether you hold an active subscription is determined on-device by StoreKit, and the answer stays on your device. We have no server that records who subscribed or for how long. For how Apple handles purchase information, please see Apple's own privacy policy.

Signing in

The app offers Sign in with Apple to establish your performer identity on this device. Signing in is optional and skipping it does not restrict any feature. That identity information is kept only on your device and is never sent to any server — we have no server to receive it. You can delete your account at any time under Settings › Account, which erases all identity information on the device in one step. Your library and annotations are unaffected, because they never belonged to the account in the first place.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan printed sheet music. What you photograph is processed into a PDF on the device and placed in your own library. Images are never uploaded anywhere, and the app uses the camera for no other purpose. The system asks for camera permission the first time you scan; declining does not affect anything else in the app.

Microphone

The microphone is used only for the tuner's listening function. While it is on, the app analyses the pitch you are playing and shows it in real time. That audio is analysed in memory as it arrives — it is not recorded, not written to any file, and not uploaded; we have no server to receive it. Closing the tuner stops capture immediately. Declining microphone permission does not affect anything else in the app, and the tuner can still play reference pitches.

IMSLP

IMSLP catalogue search inside the app runs against an index bundled with the app. Searching involves no network access at all, and what you search for never leaves your device.

Only when you choose to download an entry does the app open a page on imslp.org. That is a direct interaction between you and IMSLP, governed by IMSLP's own privacy policy and terms; we do not handle, cache or mirror their files, and we receive no record of what you do there. Once a download finishes, your device takes the file into your local library.

Third-party services

The app uses only Apple's own system services (iCloud / CloudKit sync and In-App Purchase). There is no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and no third-party account system.

Between users

The app provides no sharing, discovery, community or marketplace features between users. Your library and annotations are strictly private: no other user can discover or obtain them.

Children's privacy

Since we collect no data at all, the app collects no information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces data handling that needs explaining (an optional collaboration feature, for instance), we will update this page and say so clearly in the app's release notes.
